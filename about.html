About Us

We are five Grade 9 scholars who founded Asclepius Archives with a shared commitment to promoting awareness about the world around us—from how we interact with our ecosystems and evolve, to the science of genomes and biotechnology, and the roles of microbiomes and pathogens. We chose the name Asclepius after the Greek god of medicine and healing, a symbol of our dedication to scientific understanding and public well-being. The word “Archives” represents our mission to collect, preserve, and share reliable knowledge, making complex scientific ideas accessible to everyone.
